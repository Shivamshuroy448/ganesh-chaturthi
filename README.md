# 🪔 Happy Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 — 3D Virtual Temple Darshan, Aarti & Prayer Offering

An authentic, interactive 3D temple mandapam website celebrating **Ganesh Chaturthi** featuring Lord Ganesha on a golden throne, realistic brass lamps, sacred Aarti near the lotus feet, sweet Laddu Prasadam, realistic 3D botanical flower shower, devotional song player, and a sacred **"Write Your Wish" (Prayer Offering)** feature.

🌐 **Live Website**: [https://roysvinayakachaviti.vercel.app](https://roysvinayakachaviti.vercel.app)  
📦 **Repository**: [https://github.com/Shivamshuroy448/ganesh-chaturthi](https://github.com/Shivamshuroy448/ganesh-chaturthi)

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **✍️ Write Your Wish & Prayer Offering**:
  - Sacred Golden Palm Leaf scroll modal to write your heartfelt prayers or choose quick wishes (*Family Health & Joy*, *Career & Job Growth*, *Studies & Exams*, *Peace & Harmony*).
  - Offering your wish rings temple bells, showers flowers, and places your prayer scroll reverently at **Lord Ganesha's lotus feet** (`#offeredScrollBadge`).
  - Personalized divine blessing card with a 1-tap **WhatsApp Share** button.
  - Persists in browser `localStorage` across visits.

- **🌸 Realistic Botanical Flower Shower**:
  - Procedural botanical petal physics with 4 sacred Indian puja flowers:
    - **Marigold Petals**: Ruffled 3-lobed fan shape with saffron-orange to golden-yellow gradients.
    - **Red Rose Petals**: Velvety cupped heart petals with ruby crimson gradients and translucent curl edges.
    - **Sacred Lotus Petals**: Pointed lanceolate petals with ivory base to magenta-pink tips.
    - **Jasmine Blossoms**: 5-petal star blossom with golden center.
  - 3D aerodynamic tumble (`rotX`, `rotY`), sinusoidal air drift, and realistic settling at Lord Ganesha's feet.

- **🪔 Sacred Aarti Diya Lamp Near Lotus Feet**:
  - Small, authentic brass Panch Aarti lamp waving in a circular motion near Lord Ganesha's feet with dynamic warm lighting and real-time devotional chants.

- **🥟 Grand Vinayaka Boondi Laddu Prasadam**:
  - Traditional festive boondi laddu offering with cashews and saffron that ascends to Lord Ganesha and gracefully disappears after 5 seconds with divine blessings.

- **🔔 High-Fidelity Temple Bell Audio**:
  - Authentic acoustic temple brass bells with multi-tone resonance for left and right bells.

- **🎵 Traditional Devotional Song Player**:
  - Seamless background YouTube player for traditional Ganesh Aarti (*Lalbaugcha Raja*).

- **📱 100% Zero-Scroll Responsive Design**:
  - Perfectly fitted single-screen experience across all screen sizes (iPhone, Android, tablet, and desktop) using dynamic `visualViewport` height.

```bash

cd ganesh-chaturthi

# Start a local HTTP server
python3 -m http.server 8080

# Open in browser:
# http://localhost:8080
```
